04 / Research & publications

Research, in print.

Alongside building software, I've contributed to published engineering research.

2026

Published conference paper / Co-author

Low-Cost Hybrid Pyranometer with Machine Learning-Enhanced Solar Irradiance Sensing

Peter Eze, Rejoice Chigbo, Jerry Edeani, et al. · Engineering Proceedings 2026, 163, 54 · MDPI · Published 29 October 2026

We investigated whether a low-cost optical and thermal sensor could measure solar irradiance accurately when calibrated with machine learning. The study paired a BH1750 light sensor and dual thermistors with an ESP32 and evaluated models on more than 53,000 field observations.

Role: Co-author. The research combines sensor design, field measurements, data analysis and machine learning.
